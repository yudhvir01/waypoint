# Delete your Waypoint account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, from the app
or the website.

## From the app or the website

1. Open Waypoint and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. At the bottom, choose **Delete account…**, type `delete`, and confirm.

## What gets deleted

- **Email account (Supabase):** your account, every track, topic, task,
  note, flashcard, focus session and daily line, and every image and audio
  file you attached. This happens straight away and can't be undone. We
  keep no copy.
- **Google Drive:** the "Waypoint" folder in your Drive (your data file and
  attachments) moves to your Drive trash, which Google empties after 30
  days, and Waypoint's access to your Google account is removed.
- **Guest:** everything stored in that browser or on that phone.

If you'd like a copy first, use **Settings → Your data → Download a backup**
before deleting.
